\begin{minipage}{\textwidth}\footnotesize
Mean $\pm$ SD (sample SD) of the C-index across the five outer test folds of nested cross-validation (n = 238). $\Delta$C-index $= C_{\mathrm{fusion}} - C_{m}$, where $C_{m}$ is the C-index of model $m$ and $C_{\mathrm{fusion}}$ that of the proposed model in the same outer test fold, averaged over the five folds. All strategies combine the same clinical--report encoder and image encoder trained within each outer training set. Fusion level, the stage at which the two encoders are combined: feature, encoder features are combined and a Cox layer is trained on top (hyperparameters selected in the inner folds); risk score, the two encoder risk scores are combined by a Cox model fitted to inner out-of-fold scores. GMU, encoder features weighted by patient-specific modality gates. Gated late fusion, late-fusion coefficients multiplied by patient-specific gates computed from the two risk scores; identical to late fusion when the gate parameters are zero. Inner cross-validation turned the gates practically off in 2 of 5 folds for both OS and PFS. GMU = gated multimodal unit, OS = overall survival, PFS = progression-free survival, SD = standard deviation
