\ifshownotes\par\vspace{3pt}\footnotesize\noindent Mean $\pm$ SD (sample SD) of the C-index across the five outer test folds of nested cross-validation (n = 238). $\Delta$C-index $= C_{\mathrm{fusion}} - C_{m}$, where $C_{m}$ is the C-index of model $m$ and $C_{\mathrm{fusion}}$ that of the proposed model in the same outer test fold, averaged over the five folds. Fusion level: feature, encoder features are combined and a Cox layer is trained on top (hyperparameters selected in the inner folds); output, standardized risk scores are combined with coefficients estimated from inner out-of-fold scores. Combination: three-way, clinical (C), report (R) and image (I) inputs are combined separately (feature level: C and R features from the jointly trained clinical--report encoder; output level: risk scores of the clinical MLP, report RadBERT and image Simple CNN models); two-way, the jointly trained clinical--report model [C + R] and the image model I. GMU, encoder features weighted by patient-specific modality gates. Gated late fusion, late-fusion coefficients multiplied by patient-specific gates computed from the risk scores (penalty selected in the inner folds); hybrid fusion, the two-way gated late fusion. Inner cross-validation turned the gates practically off in 2 (OS) and 2 (PFS) of 5 folds for the hybrid fusion and in 1 (OS) and 1 (PFS) for the three-way gated late fusion. GMU = gated multimodal unit, OS = overall survival, PFS = progression-free survival, SD = standard deviation\par\fi
